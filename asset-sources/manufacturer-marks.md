# Manufacturer marks

Symbol-only manufacturer marks used by the simulation terminal. These are kept
separate from the full company wordmarks used elsewhere on the site.

- `rtx.png`, `general-dynamics.png`, and `kongsberg.png` were retrieved from the
  corresponding company domain through Logo.dev on 2026-08-05. No Logo.dev
  credential is stored in this repository.
- `lockheed-martin.png` is the transparent standalone symbol published by
  CompaniesLogo.com.
- `boeing.svg` is the standalone Boeing symbol, sourced from the public Boeing
  icon published by SVGViewer.dev.

All marks remain trademarks of their respective owners and are used here only
to identify the represented manufacturer.

- `northrop-grumman.png` reuses the existing Northrop Grumman company mark from
  `public/logos/northrop-grumman.png`.
